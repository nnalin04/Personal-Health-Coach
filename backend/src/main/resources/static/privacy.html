Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 2026

Personal Health Coach ("we", "us", or "our") is committed to protecting your personal health data. This policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and your rights.

1. Data We Collect

  • Account data — email address, hashed password (or Google OAuth token)
  • Health metrics — weight, body fat, muscle mass, steps, heart rate, BMI
  • Nutrition data — meal logs, calorie and macronutrient tracking
  • Workout data — exercise sessions, sets, reps, volume
  • Medical data — lab report PDFs and extracted lab values (uploaded voluntarily)
  • Profile data — age, gender, height, health goals, dietary preferences

2. How We Use Your Data

  • Generate AI-powered health summaries and personalized recommendations
  • Track your progress over time and display trends
  • Authenticate your account securely

We do not sell your data to third parties. We do not use your health data for advertising.

3. AI Processing

Health insights are generated using Google Gemini AI. Summaries of your aggregated health data (not raw personal identifiers) are sent to Gemini to produce recommendations. Google's data processing is governed by the Google Privacy Policy.

4. Data Storage & Security

Your data is stored in a PostgreSQL database hosted on Google Cloud Platform (GCP) in the United States. All data is encrypted in transit (TLS 1.3) and at rest. Access requires JWT authentication.

5. Your Rights

  • Export — Download all your data via Profile → Export My Data
  • Deletion — Permanently delete your account and all data via Profile → Delete Account. Deletion is irreversible and completes within 24 hours.
  • Correction — Update any profile or health data directly in the app

6. Data Retention

We retain your data for as long as your account is active. When you delete your account, all associated data is permanently removed from our servers and backups within 30 days.

7. Children's Privacy

This app is not intended for users under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

8. Changes to This Policy

We will notify you of material changes via the app or email. Continued use after changes constitutes acceptance.

9. Contact

Questions or data requests: privacy@healthcoach.app
